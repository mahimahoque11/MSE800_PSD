Here is a realistic, mid-range 7-day itinerary designed for a first-time visitor. This plan balances New Zealand’s natural landscapes, Māori culture, museums, and local culinary experiences. 

To remain within the **NZD 250–350 daily budget**, local transport, activities, and dining options have been selected with moderation in mind. Please note that the daily cost estimates exclude major domestic flights (Auckland to Rotorua, Rotorua to Wellington, and Wellington to Queenstown), which are best booked well in advance to secure the best rates.

### Key Planning Notes:
* **Accommodation:** To maintain a moderate budget, look for centrally located 3-star motels, serviced apartments, or private rooms in highly rated hostels (such as YHA/Haka Lodge chains).
* **Booking Domestic Flights:** To keep costs reasonable, domestic flights on Air New Zealand or Jetstar should be booked several months in advance, especially during the peak travel seasons (December to March).
* **Milford Sound Day Trip:** The road to Milford Sound is highly scenic but challenging to drive. Utilizing an organized coach tour from Queenstown is generally safer, reduces driver fatigue, and includes informative commentary on local geography and flora.
