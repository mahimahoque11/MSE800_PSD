Role:

Instructions:

Input Data:

- Cities to visit:
  - Auckland

Output Format:

Day | Destination | Morning | Afternoon | Evening | Transport | Estimated Daily Cost (NZD)

- Breakfast recommendation
- Lunch recommendation
- Dinner recommendation
- Major attractions

Negative Prompt:
